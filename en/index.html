Appletaco Privacy Policy

Effective date: October 6, 2026

Appletaco (the "app") is a camera app that guides your framing and applies filters when you take photos of people. The app's developer (the "developer") publishes this policy in accordance with the Personal Information Protection Act of Korea.

The app has no sign-up or login, and the developer runs no servers. The app has no internet permission, so it cannot connect to the internet on its own. All information described below is processed only on your phone, and the developer cannot receive or see it.

1. Purposes of processing personal information

  • Taking and saving photos: saves the photos you take to your phone.
  • Framing guide and closed-eye check: finds the position of faces and bodies in the camera view and in photos you take, to guide your framing and let you know when eyes look closed.
  • Changing filters: keeps the original before a filter is applied, so you can change the filter after you shoot.
  • Location: records where a photo was taken in the photo file, only if you turn this on.
  • Trial and purchase check: counts the photos saved during the trial and checks whether you bought the app on Google Play.
  • Answering inquiries: replies when you contact the developer by email.

2. Personal information processed

  • Camera view and photos you take (including faces in them): the position of faces and bodies is used only while the image is being processed and is not stored.
  • Capture info in the photo file (EXIF): records the device maker and model, time taken, app version, chosen filter, camera settings such as zoom and exposure, guide type, and closed-eye check results (the number of faces in the photo and eye-openness values). The position coordinates of faces are not recorded.
  • Location (optional): records your location at the moment of capture in the photo file (EXIF) only if you turn on 'Save location in photos' in Settings. It is off by default, and location is not stored anywhere else.
  • Originals kept for changing filters: kept in app-only storage that other apps cannot read.
  • App settings and usage status: settings, favorites, the filter history of each photo, the number of photos saved during the trial, whether the trial notice was shown, and a purchase check value are stored in app-only storage.
  • Inquiry information (optional): the email address and message you send when you contact the developer by email.

The app asks for the following permissions.

  • Camera (required): used to take photos and guide your framing. You cannot use the app without it.
  • Location (optional, approximate and precise): requested only when you turn on 'Save location in photos'. You can take photos without allowing it.
  • Billing (Google Play Billing): used for in-app purchases.

Photos (optional): by default, the app's album shows only photos taken with this app. The app asks for photo access only when you turn on 'Taken with earlier Appletaco versions' or 'All photos on this device' in Settings. Photos it reads are used only to show them in the album and do not leave your phone, except when you share them to an app you choose. If you don't allow it, you can still take photos and view photos taken with this app.

3. Processing and retention period

  • Photos you take: stay in the Pictures/Appletaco folder on your phone until you delete them. They stay even if you uninstall the app.
  • Originals kept for changing filters: there is a limit on how many are kept and how much space they use, and the oldest are deleted first when the limit is exceeded. The limit is shown on the app's Settings screen. They are deleted when you delete them, when you delete that photo in the album, or when you uninstall the app.
  • Settings, favorites, and usage status: kept until you clear the app's data or uninstall the app.
  • Location: exists only inside the photo file and is deleted together with the photo.
  • Inquiry information: destroyed without delay after the inquiry is handled.

4. Provision of personal information to third parties

The developer does not provide personal information to third parties.

If you use the share feature to send a photo to another app or person, the capture info in the photo file (including location, if you turned it on) may be sent with it. This happens only to the destination you choose.

5. Procedure and method of destruction

  • Data in app-only storage (originals, settings, usage status) is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear the app's data in Android Settings.
  • You can delete all originals kept for changing filters at any time in the app under Settings > 'Delete kept originals', and the app deletes the oldest first when the limit is exceeded.
  • You can delete photos you took in the app's album or in your phone's gallery app.
  • Inquiry emails are permanently deleted from the mailbox when the retention period ends.
  • No personal information is kept separately under any law.

6. Outsourcing of personal information processing

The developer does not outsource the processing of personal information.

Payments for in-app purchases are processed by Google Play. The app does not receive payment information such as card numbers, and only checks through Google Play whether you made a purchase. Payment information is handled under Google's Privacy Policy (policies.google.com/privacy).

7. Rights and obligations of users and legal representatives, and how to exercise them

You can ask at any time to access, correct, or delete your personal information, or to stop processing it. Because all information the app handles is on your phone, you can exercise these rights directly as follows.

  • Stop recording location: turn off Settings > 'Save location in photos', or revoke location permission in Android Settings.
  • Stop keeping originals and delete them: turn off Settings > 'Keep originals to change filters', then tap 'Delete kept originals'.
  • Delete photos: delete them in the app's album or in a gallery app.
  • Delete all app data: clear the app's data in Android Settings or uninstall the app.

For rights regarding inquiry information, you can make a request to the contact below, and the developer will act without delay. Legal representatives of children under 14 can exercise these rights in the same way.

8. Installation, operation, and refusal of tools that collect personal information automatically

The app does not use tools that collect personal information automatically, such as cookies, advertising IDs, or analytics tools.

Google's ML Kit (face detection) and MediaPipe (body detection) libraries included in the app have a feature that sends usage statistics such as device model, app version, and processing time to Google. Because the app has no internet permission, these statistics are not sent from the app. Photos and face images are not part of these statistics.

To find problems, the app writes short diagnostic records (camera opening, processing time, error type) only to the phone's system log. They contain no photos, face positions, or location, and are not sent off the device.

9. Measures to keep personal information safe

  • Blocking transfer off the device: the internet permission has been removed from the app, so the app cannot send information off the device.
  • Access restriction: originals kept for changing filters and settings are stored in app-only storage that other apps cannot read.
  • Excluded from backup: app data is excluded from Google account backup and from transfer to a new device.
  • Minimal processing: the position of faces and bodies is used only during processing and is not stored. Location is recorded only when you turn it on.

10. Person in charge of personal information protection

  • Person in charge of personal information protection: the app developer
  • Contact: The developer contact on the Appletaco page in Google Play

Please send questions, complaints, or requests for remedies about personal information to the contact above, and you will get a reply without delay.

11. Remedies for infringement of rights

You can get advice or report an infringement of personal information to the following agencies in Korea.

  • Personal Information Infringement Report Center: 118 without an area code (privacy.kisa.or.kr)
  • Personal Information Dispute Mediation Committee: 1833-6972 (www.kopico.go.kr)
  • Supreme Prosecutors' Office: 1301 without an area code (www.spo.go.kr)
  • Korean National Police Agency: 182 without an area code (ecrm.police.go.kr)

12. Changes to this privacy policy

If this policy changes, the changes and their effective date will be announced on this page and in the in-app policy starting 7 days before they take effect. Changes that significantly affect your rights will be announced starting 30 days before.
